# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume the other person is acting in good faith. Criticise code, not
people.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments, publishing anyone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussion.

## Scope

Applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing this project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainer through
[private vulnerability reporting](https://github.com/mithudso/youtube-transcript-to-pdf/security/advisories/new)
or a direct message. Reports stay confidential. The maintainer may edit or
delete comments, close threads, or block accounts.

This is adapted from the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
kept short because this is a small project.
